Common questions

Where is my data? On your iPhone. There is no INDEX account or app-managed cloud sync. Device backups are managed separately by Apple and your settings.

How do I export it? Settings → Export data → JSON + media or Markdown + media. Save the ZIP to a location you control. Exports contain saved records and media, not drafts. The current version does not import or restore export packages.

Why can't I save media? Each record supports up to 9 items. Each file must be no larger than 200 MB, and each video no longer than 3 minutes. Wait for preparation to finish; retry or remove failed items. Make sure the device has enough free storage.

Can I use the app without camera access? Yes. Text entry remains available. You can change camera/microphone access in iOS Settings and choose items through the system photo picker.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Data management. Export anything you want to keep first. The deletion cannot be undone and does not remove external exports or device backups. iOS Offload App retains data; Delete App removes the local app container.

Can support retrieve my deleted notes? No. The developer has no server copy of your workspace. Avoid deleting the app when troubleshooting; contact support first.
