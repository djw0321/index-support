INDEX Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy describes INDEX: Notes to Continue (displayed on your device as INDEX), an iPhone app for projects, entries and handoffs. It is provided by the developer of INDEX. Contact: 345785436@qq.com.

Your workspace

Projects, entry titles and text, handoffs, selected photos and videos, drafts, and app preferences are stored in the app's local storage. INDEX does not operate an account system or an app-managed cloud sync service. It does not send your workspace to the developer's servers. The current app includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Your device may include app data in Apple-managed device backups, depending on your settings. This is separate from an INDEX cloud sync service. See your Apple device and backup settings to manage those copies.

Camera, microphone and photo selection

INDEX asks for camera access when you choose to take a photo or record a video, and microphone access when needed to record video sound. You can decline or change permissions in iOS Settings and continue using text entry and other available features. The system photo picker gives INDEX the items you select; INDEX does not scan your entire photo library.

Imported media may retain metadata such as capture time, location or device information if present in the original file. Local previews and file checksums support viewing, file validation and exports. INDEX does not use these to identify or track you.

Retention and deletion

Saved content and drafts remain locally until you edit or delete them, or remove the app. You can delete individual records or use Settings → Data management to delete all saved records, drafts, media and app migration backups. Older migration backups may retain a prior copy until you use Delete all data or remove the app. File cleanup may be retried if storage operations fail. Removing the app with Delete App removes its local container; iOS Offload App retains documents and data.

Exports, media you share elsewhere, and device backups are separate copies. Deleting data in INDEX does not delete those copies. INDEX cannot recover data after it has been removed from local storage. Keep an external export before deleting data or changing devices; the current version does not offer in-app export-package restoration.

Exports, external links and support

When you choose Export data, INDEX prepares a ZIP with saved records and original media, including available metadata. Drafts are excluded. You choose whether and where to share or save it through iOS. The selected destination handles its copy under its own policies. External links open using the system browser.

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and anything you choose to send, and uses them to respond and troubleshoot. Do not send sensitive notes, private photos, passwords or full library exports unless necessary. You can request deletion of support correspondence by email, subject to any legally required retention. Your email provider and the support mailbox provider also process email under their policies.

This support website

This website is prepared for hosting on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in. This website includes no advertising, analytics scripts, or contact forms. Visiting these pages does not upload your INDEX workspace. See GitHub’s privacy statement for the hosting provider’s practices.

Your choices and changes

You can change permissions, edit or delete local content, export saved records, or contact the developer at 345785436@qq.com. No account deletion request is required because INDEX has no account system. Material changes to this policy will be reflected in an updated date and, when appropriate, an in-app notice.

INDEX 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日

本政策适用于 INDEX: Notes to Continue（设备上显示为 INDEX），一款记录项目、Entry 与 Handoff 的 iPhone 应用。由 INDEX 开发者提供，联系邮箱：345785436@qq.com。

本地工作记录

项目、标题和正文、交接、选中的照片与视频、草稿及偏好设置保存在应用本地。INDEX 没有账号体系或应用自营云同步服务，不向开发者服务器上传你的工作记录；当前应用不含广告或第三方统计 SDK，不跨应用或网站追踪你。

根据设备设置，Apple 管理的设备备份可能包含应用数据。这与 INDEX 云同步不同，请在 Apple 的设备与备份设置中管理这些副本。

权限与媒体

仅当你选择拍照或录像时，应用才按需请求相机与麦克风权限。拒绝授权不影响文字记录等可用功能；权限可在 iOS 设置中调整。系统图库选择器只向 INDEX 提供你选中的项目，应用不会扫描整个图库。

原媒体可能保留拍摄时间、位置、设备信息等元数据。预览与文件校验信息用于本地查看、完整性检查及导出，不用于识别或追踪你。

保存、删除与备份

内容和草稿保留在本地，直到你编辑、删除或删除应用。可以单独删除记录，也可以在 Settings → Data management 删除全部记录、草稿、媒体与应用迁移备份。历史迁移备份可能保留早期内容，直到执行删除全部数据或删除应用；文件清理失败时可能需要重试。

iOS 的“删除 App”移除本地应用容器，“卸载 App”则保留文稿与数据。导出文件、分享至其他位置的媒体以及设备备份属于独立副本，应用内删除不会删除它们。开发者无法替你找回已从本地移除的数据。删除或换机前请保留外部导出；当前版本尚无应用内导出包恢复功能。

导出、外部链接与支持

导出 ZIP 包含已保存的记录、原始媒体及可能存在的元数据，不含草稿。你通过系统分享界面选择保存或分享的目标，目标服务按其自身政策处理副本。外部链接通过系统浏览器打开。

主动发送支持邮件时，开发者会收到你的邮箱地址及主动提供的信息，仅用于回复与排障。请勿非必要地发送敏感笔记、私人照片、密码或完整数据包。可以通过邮件请求删除支持通信记录，依法需要保留的情形除外；电子邮件服务商也按各自政策处理邮件。

支持网站

本网站准备使用 GitHub Pages 托管。无论访问者是否登录，GitHub 都会出于安全目的记录并保存访问者的 IP 地址。网站不含广告、统计脚本或在线表单；访问网页不会上传你的 INDEX 工作记录。托管服务的数据处理方式见 GitHub 隐私声明。

联系与政策更新

你可以管理权限、编辑或删除本地记录、导出已保存内容，或联系 345785436@qq.com。因没有账号体系，无需办理账号注销。政策更新会修改上述日期，重大变更会视情况在应用内提示。
